Internal links are one of the few ranking levers you fully control. They shape how both users and search engines move through your site.

## Why internal links matter

- They help crawlers discover new and deep pages
- They pass authority from strong pages to weaker ones
- They signal which pages are most important

## Topic clusters

Group related content around a central pillar page. The pillar links out to detailed subtopic posts, and each post links back. This structure shows search engines you cover a subject in depth.

## Write descriptive anchors

Anchor text tells Google what the linked page is about. Use natural, specific phrases rather than generic ones like "this post" or "learn more."

## Watch for orphan pages

An orphan page has no internal links pointing to it. Crawlers struggle to find it, and it receives no internal authority. Audit regularly with a crawler like Screaming Frog.

## Keep it natural

Link where it genuinely helps the reader. A handful of relevant links beats dozens of forced ones stuffed into every paragraph.
